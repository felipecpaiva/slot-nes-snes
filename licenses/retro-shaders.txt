The NES and SNES screen shaders Sharp, Scanlines, crt-pi and crt-1tap are ports of shaders
from the libretro shader collection
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders
to one GLSL ES 1.00 single-pass program (crates/slot-gfx/src/pipeline/retro.rs in the patched
slot source). The game texture stays nearest-filtered, so where a shader expects bilinear
filtering the port samples bilinearly by hand with 4 texture reads.

Sharp: sharp-bilinear-simple by rsn8887, based on TheMaister's sharp-bilinear.
interpolation/shaders/sharp-bilinear-simple.glsl. Public domain.

Scanlines: Scanlines Sine Absolute Value by RiskyJumps.
scanlines/shaders/scanlines-sine-abs.glsl. Public domain.

crt-pi: crt/shaders/crt-pi.glsl.
