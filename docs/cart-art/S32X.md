# Sega 32X cart art (issue 151)

Same rules as [VARIANTS.md](VARIANTS.md): every value cites its source, or says it is the
maintainer's choice. Nothing is guessed.

## The cart

One cart for every 32X game, `RetroShell::S32x` (patch 0097).

| Value | Ours | Source |
|---|---|---|
| One cart for every region, no end label | one shell, no region variants | Sega Retro, [Sega 32X cartridges](https://segaretro.org/Sega_32X_cartridges), checked 2026-10-20: "While the dimensions are similar, the shape differs with the 32X and (labels aside) the design is consistent across all regions", "there are also no end labels" |
| Size | the Mega Drive cart's: seated 240 x 164, drawn 360 x 246 | same page ("the dimensions are similar"); the exact size is the maintainer's choice, the Genesis cart's box (ADR 0056) |
| Outline | rounded top corners (r 10.2 of 240), square-ish bottom (r 3) | ours: the top of the 32X shell differs from the Genesis cart; the radius is the Mega Drive cart's top radius (`megadrive_cart.svg`) |
| Relief | a groove under a top cap (y 13.6 to 15 of 164) and the Genesis cart's ridged sides | ours, drawn as `s32x_cart_detail.svg` from `genesis_cart_detail.svg` |
| Label well | permille 166, 140, 836, 909 (241 x 190 px) | the Genesis well (ADR 0056), moved below the cap |

Drawn by us as `crates/slot-ui/assets/s32x_cart.svg` (outline) and `s32x_cart_detail.svg` (relief),
lit into `assets/retro/s32x_neutral.png` by `examples/retro_neutrals.rs`. Every other neutral
regenerates byte for byte (sha256 compared before and after).

## Shell colour

| Carts | Colour | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Every 32X cart | `26 24 2a`, solid | Maintainer's choice (ADR 0096): 32X carts are black plastic like the Mega Drive's, so they take `MEGA_DRIVE_BLACK`'s measured value. The yellow 32X band is printed on the label, and comes with the label art |

A colour in `Config/cart_shell.txt` still overrides it.

## Labels

`tools/fetch-labels.py`, system `32X`: dat `Sega - 32X` (libretro-database `metadat/no-intro`);
ScreenScraper `sega32x` 19 (Skyscraper `platforms_idmap.csv` at ccd49ae, row `sega32x,19,21,33`). A
name search drops the word 32X from names like `Doom 32X (JU) [!]`. There are no libretro-thumbnails
`Sega - 32X` Named_Logos (404, 2026-10-20), so the Logo Only fallback uses the ScreenScraper wheel.
